What We Build Around.

MLC is a working environment for students who want to understand models deeply, test ideas carefully, and build things that can survive outside a classroom.

The Machine Learning Club at Aligarh Muslim University creates a collaborative environment where students explore artificial intelligence, work on meaningful projects, and transform theoretical knowledge into practical systems.

Our goal is to help students develop strong technical foundations, research habits, and real-world project experience.
