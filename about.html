About Us

The Machine Learning Club at Aligarh Muslim University is a student-led community exploring machine learning, artificial intelligence, and data science.

We learn by building through projects, research, workshops, competitions, and collaborative exploration.

Our goal is to build a culture where curiosity becomes capability—where students learn deeply, experiment freely, and turn ideas into meaningful intelligent systems.
